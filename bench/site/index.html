Why patients choose Harbourline

Every new patient has a forty-minute first appointment with a dentist. We use that time to understand what matters to you, check your teeth and gums thoroughly and explain what we find in plain language before anything is booked.

Each treatment plan is written down with the fee for every item. You can take it away, think it over and ask questions by email or phone. We never ask for a decision in the chair.

Our clinics hold emergency appointments every weekday morning, so existing patients in pain are seen the same day. Evening appointments run until 7pm in Bristol, and Bath opens at 7.30am on Tuesdays.

Three clinics

Our Bristol clinic on Quay Street is the largest and hosts the implant service. The Bath clinic at Abbey Green focuses on aligners and cosmetic dentistry. Our Leeds clinic at Granary Wharf leads our gum health work.

New to Harbourline?

Your first visit is a forty-minute appointment with a dentist and costs £75, including any X-rays. We ask about your medical history, check your teeth, gums and the soft tissues of your mouth, and explain what we find. You leave with a written plan and fees, and there is never any pressure to start treatment that day.

If you have been away from the dentist for a long time, you are not alone and you will not be lectured. Tell us when you book and we will allow extra time.
