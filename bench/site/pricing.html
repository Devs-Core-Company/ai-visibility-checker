Our prices

We publish all our fees. Your dentist will give you a written plan with the exact cost before any treatment begins.

What is included

Examination fees include any routine X-rays and a written treatment plan. Treatment fees include local anaesthetic and a review appointment where needed. Laboratory fees for crowns, veneers and dentures are included in the prices shown.

Paying for treatment

Payment is due at each appointment. For courses of treatment over £1,000 you can pay a deposit and spread the rest over twelve months interest-free, subject to status. Read more on our finance page.

Dental insurance

We provide itemised receipts for insurance claims. We do not claim directly from insurers, so you pay us and then claim back from your provider.

Why we publish our fees

We think patients should be able to compare prices before booking. The table above lists our standard fees. Complex cases, such as implants with bone grafting or full-mouth treatment, are priced individually after a consultation, and the written plan you receive sets out each stage separately. If a fee changes during a course of treatment, we explain why before continuing, and we never add charges without your agreement.

Children under eighteen are seen free for check-ups when a parent or guardian is a registered patient.
