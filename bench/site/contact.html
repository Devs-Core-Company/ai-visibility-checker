Contact us

For appointments, call the clinic you want to visit. For anything else, email hello@harbourline.example or use the form below and we will reply within one working day.

Phone lines open at 8am on weekdays. If you are in severe pain, call as early as you can so we can offer an emergency slot the same day. Out of hours, call NHS 111 for urgent dental advice.

What not to send by email

Please do not send photographs of your mouth or detailed medical information through the form. If we need them, we will send you a secure link. Messages are read by our reception team during opening hours.

Writing to us

Letters can be sent to the practice manager at Harbourline Dental Studio, 14 Quay Street, Bristol BS1 4DB. We aim to reply to letters within five working days. For questions about an invoice, please quote the reference shown on your receipt so that we can find your record quickly.

If your question is about a complaint, please see our complaints procedure, which explains how we handle concerns and how quickly we respond.
