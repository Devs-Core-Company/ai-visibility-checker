How to brush your teeth

By Tom Ellis, Dental therapist · Published 23 February 2026 · Updated 10 March 2026

Key points from this article

  • Before you start: Use a soft or medium brush with a small head and a fluoride toothpaste of 1,350 to 1,500 ppm.
  • Step by step: Angle the bristles at forty-five degrees towards the gum line.
  • Finish properly: Spit out the toothpaste but do not rinse.
  • Common problems: If your gums bleed, keep brushing gently; bleeding usually stops within two weeks as the gums heal.

Before you start

Use a soft or medium brush with a small head and a fluoride toothpaste of 1,350 to 1,500 ppm. Put a pea-sized amount on the brush.

Step by step

Angle the bristles at forty-five degrees towards the gum line. Work in small circles on each tooth, following the same order every time: outer surfaces, inner surfaces, then chewing surfaces, top then bottom.

Spend about thirty seconds on each quarter of your mouth, two minutes in total.

Finish properly

Spit out the toothpaste but do not rinse. Brush last thing at night and on one other occasion during the day.

Common problems

If your gums bleed, keep brushing gently; bleeding usually stops within two weeks as the gums heal. If it does not, see your hygienist.
