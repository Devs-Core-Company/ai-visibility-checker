Refurbished surgeries in Bristol

By Dr Amelia Hart, Principal dentist · Published 18 August 2026

Key points from this article

  • What has changed: Surgeries one and two have new dental chairs, ceiling-mounted screens so you can watch something calming during treatment, and quieter suction units.
  • Decontamination: A new decontamination room with a washer-disinfector and two vacuum autoclaves means instruments are cleaned and sterilised away from the treatment rooms, as recommended in national guidance.
  • Accessibility: Both rooms now have wider doors, and surgery two can accommodate a patient who prefers to stay in a wheelchair for a check-up.
  • Thank you: Thank you to everyone who put up with the noise during the four-week works.

What has changed

Surgeries one and two have new dental chairs, ceiling-mounted screens so you can watch something calming during treatment, and quieter suction units.

Decontamination

A new decontamination room with a washer-disinfector and two vacuum autoclaves means instruments are cleaned and sterilised away from the treatment rooms, as recommended in national guidance.

Thank you

Thank you to everyone who put up with the noise during the four-week works. Appointments are back to normal hours.

About the author. Dr Amelia Hart is a principal dentist at Harbourline Dental Studio. Amelia qualified from the University of Bristol in 2007 and worked in NHS practice in Weston-super-Mare before opening Harbourline on Quay Street in 2012.
