Tooth-friendly snacks for children

By Tom Ellis, Dental therapist

Key points from this article

  • Good choices: Cheese, plain yoghurt, vegetable sticks with hummus, plain popcorn for older children, wholemeal toast and fresh fruit eaten with a meal are all kind to teeth.
  • Snacks to limit: Fruit juice, squash, biscuits, cereal bars and dried fruit are common in lunchboxes but are high in sugar or stick to the teeth.
  • Timing: Children need regular meals and one or two planned snacks, not constant grazing.
  • After-school ideas: A slice of cheese on an oatcake, a boiled egg or a handful of cherry tomatoes will hold most children until tea.

Good choices

Cheese, plain yoghurt, vegetable sticks with hummus, plain popcorn for older children, wholemeal toast and fresh fruit eaten with a meal are all kind to teeth. Water and plain milk are the only drinks children need between meals.

Snacks to limit

Fruit juice, squash, biscuits, cereal bars and dried fruit are common in lunchboxes but are high in sugar or stick to the teeth. Keep them to mealtimes and as occasional treats.

Timing

Children need regular meals and one or two planned snacks, not constant grazing. A predictable routine gives saliva time to repair the teeth between eating.

After-school ideas

A slice of cheese on an oatcake, a boiled egg or a handful of cherry tomatoes will hold most children until tea. If they want something sweet, offer it straight after a meal.
