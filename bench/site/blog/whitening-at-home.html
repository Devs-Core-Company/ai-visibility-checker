Keeping your whitening results

By Dr Sophie Lang, Orthodontic and cosmetic dentist · Published 2 June 2026 · Updated 10 June 2026

Key points from this article

  • Top-up treatments: Keep your whitening trays.
  • Food and drink: Coffee, tea, red wine, cola and curry stain teeth most.
  • Toothpaste: A normal fluoride toothpaste is all you need.
  • Smoking: Tobacco stains teeth quickly after whitening.

Top-up treatments

Keep your whitening trays. One or two nights of top-up wear every six to twelve months maintains most people’s results. Store the gel in the fridge and check the expiry date.

Food and drink

Coffee, tea, red wine, cola and curry stain teeth most. You do not need to give them up; rinse with water afterwards and drink cold drinks through a straw.

Toothpaste

A normal fluoride toothpaste is all you need. Whitening toothpastes remove some surface stain but can be abrasive, so use them only a few times a week.

Smoking

Tobacco stains teeth quickly after whitening. Our team can refer you to free local stop-smoking services if you would like support.
