The usual cause

In nine out of ten cases bad breath comes from the mouth itself, from bacteria on the tongue and between the teeth breaking down food particles and releasing sulphur compounds.

Other causes

Gum disease, food trapped under a broken filling, smoking, strong foods such as garlic, and dry mouth all make breath worse. Less often, sinus or stomach problems are the cause.

What helps

Clean between your teeth daily, brush your tongue or use a tongue scraper, drink plenty of water and see the hygienist regularly. Sugar-free gum can help in the short term.

When to get advice

If bad breath continues despite good cleaning, book a check-up. If your dentist finds no cause in the mouth, they may suggest you see your GP.
