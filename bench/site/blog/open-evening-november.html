Implant open evening at our Bristol clinic

By Dr Amelia Hart, Principal dentist · Published 15 September 2026 · Updated 20 September 2026

Key points from this article

  • What to expect: Dr Rohan Mehta will give a twenty-minute talk on how implants work, who is suitable and what treatment costs, followed by time for questions.
  • When and where: Thursday 20 November 2026, 6pm to 8pm, at Harbourline Bristol, 14 Quay Street.
  • Who should come: Anyone missing one or more teeth, wearing a denture they are unhappy with, or considering implants after an extraction.
  • How to book: Book a free place by phoning the Bristol clinic or using our online booking form and choosing open evening as the reason for your visit.

What to expect

Dr Rohan Mehta will give a twenty-minute talk on how implants work, who is suitable and what treatment costs, followed by time for questions. You can also book a ten-minute private chat.

When and where

Thursday 20 November 2026, 6pm to 8pm, at Harbourline Bristol, 14 Quay Street. Light refreshments are provided. Places are limited to twenty people.

Who should come

Anyone missing one or more teeth, wearing a denture they are unhappy with, or considering implants after an extraction. Partners and family members are welcome.

How to book

Book a free place by phoning the Bristol clinic or using our online booking form and choosing open evening as the reason for your visit. We will send a reminder the day before.

If you have questions about anything in this article, our team can help at your next appointment. Read about general dentistry at Harbourline or book a visit online.

About the author. Dr Amelia Hart is a principal dentist at Harbourline Dental Studio. Amelia qualified from the University of Bristol in 2007 and worked in NHS practice in Weston-super-Mare before opening Harbourline on Quay Street in 2012.
