Help for nervous dental patients

By Dr Amelia Hart, Principal dentist · Published 3 November 2025 · Updated 10 January 2026

Key points from this article

  • You are not alone: Around one in four adults in the UK say they feel anxious about visiting the dentist, and about one in ten avoid it altogether.
  • What we change for you: Your first visit can be a conversation in a consultation room rather than the dental chair.
  • Sedation: For some people, conscious sedation with a small dose of medicine through a cannula makes treatment possible.
  • Taking the first step: Phone the clinic and tell the receptionist that you are nervous.

You are not alone

Around one in four adults in the UK say they feel anxious about visiting the dentist, and about one in ten avoid it altogether. Many of our long-standing patients first came to us after years away.

Anxiety is usually rooted in a bad experience, often in childhood. We start by listening rather than by examining.

What we change for you

Your first visit can be a conversation in a consultation room rather than the dental chair. We book longer appointments so nothing feels rushed, and we agree a hand signal that means stop, which we always respect.

You can bring headphones, a friend or a weighted blanket. We explain each step before we do it and tell you how long it will take.

Sedation

For some people, conscious sedation with a small dose of medicine through a cannula makes treatment possible. You remain awake but relaxed and remember little afterwards. You need an adult to take you home and stay with you for the rest of the day.

Taking the first step

Phone the clinic and tell the receptionist that you are nervous. They will add a note to your record so every member of the team knows before you arrive, and they can book you with a dentist who works regularly with anxious patients.

If you have questions about anything in this article, our team can help at your next appointment. Read about general dentistry at Harbourline or book a visit online.

About the author. Dr Amelia Hart is a principal dentist at Harbourline Dental Studio. Amelia qualified from the University of Bristol in 2007 and worked in NHS practice in Weston-super-Mare before opening Harbourline on Quay Street in 2012.
