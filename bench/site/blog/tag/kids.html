Articles tagged children

Advice for parents and carers on looking after children’s teeth, from the first tooth to the teenage years. Tooth decay is the most common reason for young children to be admitted to hospital in England, and almost all of it is preventable. Our dental therapists and dentists write about first visits, brushing routines, snacks and drinks, and the preventive treatments such as fluoride varnish and fissure sealants that we offer at every clinic. Children are seen free when a parent is a registered patient.

Free check-ups for children

Children under eighteen are seen free for check-ups at every Harbourline clinic when a parent or guardian is a registered patient. Appointments are short, and we can book siblings back to back so the whole family is seen in one visit. Bring your child’s red book or vaccination record to the first visit if it is handy.
