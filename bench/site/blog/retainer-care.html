Caring for your retainer

By Dr Sophie Lang, Orthodontic and cosmetic dentist · Published 24 June 2026

Key points from this article

  • Removable retainers: Rinse your retainer in cool water every morning and brush it gently with a soft toothbrush and unscented soap.
  • Fixed retainers: A bonded wire behind the front teeth collects tartar.
  • Storage: Always keep a removable retainer in its case when it is not in your mouth.
  • Replacement: Removable retainers usually last two to five years.

Removable retainers

Rinse your retainer in cool water every morning and brush it gently with a soft toothbrush and unscented soap. Hot water warps the plastic, and toothpaste scratches it.

Fixed retainers

A bonded wire behind the front teeth collects tartar. Clean around it daily with floss threaders or small interdental brushes and keep your hygiene appointments.

Storage

Always keep a removable retainer in its case when it is not in your mouth. Most lost retainers are wrapped in a napkin at a restaurant and thrown away.

Replacement

Removable retainers usually last two to five years. Replace them sooner if they crack, discolour or stop fitting snugly.
