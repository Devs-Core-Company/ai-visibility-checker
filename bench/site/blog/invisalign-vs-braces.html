Clear aligners or fixed braces?

By Dr Sophie Lang, Orthodontic and cosmetic dentist · Published 12 May 2026 · Updated 20 May 2026

Key points from this article

  • Appearance and comfort: Aligners are nearly invisible and can be removed to eat and brush.
  • Which cases each suits: Aligners suit mild to moderate crowding and spacing in adults who will wear them for twenty-two hours a day.
  • Treatment time and cost: For straightforward cases both take six to eighteen months.
  • After treatment: Both need retainers for life to stop teeth moving back.

Appearance and comfort

Aligners are nearly invisible and can be removed to eat and brush. Fixed braces are visible, though tooth-coloured brackets help, and they can rub the cheeks for the first few weeks.

Which cases each suits

Aligners suit mild to moderate crowding and spacing in adults who will wear them for twenty-two hours a day. Fixed braces handle complex movements and are better when compliance may be a problem, such as for many teenagers.

Treatment time and cost

For straightforward cases both take six to eighteen months. At Harbourline aligner treatment starts at £2,900 and fixed braces at £3,200, including retainers.

After treatment

Both need retainers for life to stop teeth moving back. We fit a thin bonded wire behind the front teeth and a removable retainer to wear at night.
