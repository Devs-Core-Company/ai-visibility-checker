How much do dental implants cost?

By Dr Rohan Mehta, Implant dentist · Published 9 February 2026 · Updated 1 March 2026

Key points from this article

  • The short answer: At Harbourline a single implant with its crown costs £2,650.
  • What can add to the cost: A bone graft adds between £450 and £900 depending on the amount needed.
  • Why prices vary between practices: Fees across the UK range widely.
  • Spreading the cost: We offer interest-free finance over twelve months for treatment over £1,000, subject to status.

The short answer

At Harbourline a single implant with its crown costs £2,650. That fee covers the 3D scan, the surgical guide, the implant, the abutment, the crown and all review visits for the first year.

What can add to the cost

A bone graft adds between £450 and £900 depending on the amount needed. Extracting the old tooth on the same day adds £180. Sedation, if you want it, is £295 per visit.

Replacing several teeth with an implant bridge or an implant-retained denture costs more in total but less per tooth than individual implants.

Why prices vary between practices

Fees across the UK range widely. The differences usually come from the implant system used, whether a scan and guide are included, and how many review visits are part of the package. Always ask for a written itemised plan.

Spreading the cost

We offer interest-free finance over twelve months for treatment over £1,000, subject to status. Details are on our finance page, and our 2023 price list shows how fees have changed, and our treatment coordinator can talk you through the options.

If you have questions about anything in this article, our team can help at your next appointment. Read about general dentistry at Harbourline or book a visit online.
