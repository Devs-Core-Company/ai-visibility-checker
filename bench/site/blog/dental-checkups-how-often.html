How often do you need a check-up?

It depends on you

UK guidance from NICE says the interval between check-ups should be based on your own risk of dental disease. For adults it can range from three months to two years; for children, from three months to one year.

What decides the interval

Your dentist considers your history of decay, the health of your gums, your diet, whether you smoke, your medical history and how well you clean your teeth. These are reviewed at every visit, so the interval can change.

Why some people need more

People with gum disease, a high sugar intake, dry mouth or many fillings tend to develop new problems faster and benefit from closer monitoring.

Why some people need fewer

Adults with healthy gums, no recent decay and good home care may only need a check-up every eighteen months to two years. Hygiene visits may still be more frequent.

What we do

At Harbourline your dentist tells you your recommended interval at the end of each check-up and explains why. Reminders are sent by text and email two weeks before you are due.
