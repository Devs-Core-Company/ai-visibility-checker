Early signs of gum disease

By Priya Shah, Dental hygienist · Published 10 June 2025 · Updated 2 August 2025

What gum disease is

Gum disease is inflammation caused by plaque bacteria at the gum line. In its early form, gingivitis, it is reversible. Left untreated it can become periodontitis, which destroys the bone holding the teeth in place.

Signs to look out for

Gums that bleed when you brush or floss, red or swollen gums, persistent bad breath and a bad taste are early signs. Later signs include gums shrinking back, gaps appearing between teeth and teeth feeling loose.

Gum disease is usually painless, which is why many people do not notice it until it is advanced.

Who is at risk

Smoking is the biggest single risk factor. Diabetes, stress, pregnancy and a family history of gum disease also raise the risk.

Treatment

Treatment starts with thorough cleaning above and below the gum line, together with better cleaning at home. Most people see a big improvement within three months. Advanced cases may need specialist care.
