About cosmetic treatment at Harbourline

Cosmetic dentistry covers treatment that changes how teeth look rather than treating disease. We always check that teeth and gums are healthy first, and we favour the least invasive option that achieves what you want, such as straightening and whitening before veneers. Every cosmetic plan starts with photographs and a written estimate.
