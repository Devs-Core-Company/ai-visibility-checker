Scale and polish explained

By Priya Shah, Dental hygienist · Published 7 April 2026

A hygiene appointment removes the hard tartar that brushing cannot shift and gives you practical advice for keeping gums healthy between visits.

What happens at the appointment

The hygienist measures the gum pockets, removes tartar above and below the gum line with an ultrasonic scaler and fine hand instruments, then polishes away surface stain. Most visits take thirty to forty-five minutes.

If the gums bleed easily or the pockets are deeper than four millimetres, the hygienist will suggest a course of deeper cleaning over two or three visits.

How often to go

People with healthy gums usually need a visit every six to twelve months. Smokers, people with diabetes and anyone treated for gum disease benefit from visits every three to four months.

Advice you can use at home

Your hygienist will show you the right size of interdental brush for each gap and check your brushing technique. Small changes in technique make a larger difference than any particular brand of toothpaste.
