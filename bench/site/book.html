Book an appointment

Choose a clinic, a reason for your visit and a preferred date. We will confirm a time within one working day. If you are in pain, please phone instead.







What happens next

A receptionist checks the diary and contacts you with the nearest available time. New patients receive a medical history form by email to complete before the visit. If you need to change the time, reply to the confirmation email or call the clinic.

Choosing the right appointment

Choose check-up if you are an existing patient due a routine examination, or if you are new to the practice. Choose hygiene for a scale and polish or gum treatment with one of our hygienists. Choose consultation if you would like to discuss implants, aligners, whitening or veneers. If none of these fit, choose check-up and add a note when we call you.

Appointments requested before 3pm on a weekday are usually confirmed the same day. We will never book an appointment without speaking to you or emailing you first.

Cancelling or changing an appointment

Please give at least two working days’ notice so that we can offer the time to someone else. You can change an appointment by replying to your confirmation email or by phoning the clinic.
