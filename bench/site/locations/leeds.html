Harbourline Leeds, Granary Wharf

Our newest clinic opened in 2021 beside the canal at Granary Wharf, a short walk from Leeds railway station’s south entrance.

It has two surgeries and a hygiene room, and specialises in gum health. Priya Shah leads the hygiene team here.

Opening hours are Tuesday to Friday 8.30am to 6pm and alternate Saturdays 9am to 1pm.

Treatments at this clinic

All clinics offer check-ups and general dentistry, hygiene appointments and emergency care. Leeds also runs our gum health clinic.

Facilities at Leeds

The Leeds clinic has a fully accessible toilet, a hearing loop at reception and baby-changing facilities. Free drinking water is available in the waiting room. Card payments, including contactless, are taken at the desk.

Booking at Leeds

You can book online or by phoning the clinic on 0113 496 0784. Existing patients in pain should call from 8am for an emergency appointment the same day. New patients are welcome, and the first appointment lasts forty minutes so there is time to talk through your history and any concerns.
