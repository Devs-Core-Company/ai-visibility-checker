Harbourline Bristol, Quay Street

Our first and largest clinic has five surgeries, a dedicated hygiene room and the 3D scanner used for implant planning. It is on the ground floor with step-free access from Quay Street.

The nearest car park is Trenchard Street, about four minutes on foot. Bus routes 72, 75 and 76 stop at the Centre, two minutes away.

Opening hours are Monday to Friday 8am to 7pm and Saturday 9am to 2pm. Emergency slots are held every weekday morning.

Treatments at this clinic

All clinics offer check-ups and general dentistry, hygiene appointments and emergency care. Bristol is also home to our implant service.

Facilities at Bristol

The Bristol clinic has a fully accessible toilet, a hearing loop at reception and baby-changing facilities. Free drinking water is available in the waiting room. Card payments, including contactless, are taken at the desk.

Booking at Bristol

You can book online or by phoning the clinic on 0117 496 0123. Existing patients in pain should call from 8am for an emergency appointment the same day. New patients are welcome, and the first appointment lasts forty minutes so there is time to talk through your history and any concerns.
