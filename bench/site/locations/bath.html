Harbourline Bath, Abbey Green

The Bath clinic opened in 2016 in a restored Georgian building behind the Abbey. It has three surgeries and is the home of our clear aligner and cosmetic service.

There is no on-site parking. Southgate car park is a seven-minute walk, and Bath Spa railway station is ten minutes away on foot.

The clinic is open Monday to Thursday 7.30am to 6pm and Friday 8am to 4pm. Treatment rooms are on the first floor and there is a lift.

Treatments at this clinic

All clinics offer check-ups and general dentistry, hygiene appointments and emergency care. Bath also provides clear aligners and veneers.

Facilities at Bath

The Bath clinic has a fully accessible toilet, a hearing loop at reception and baby-changing facilities. Free drinking water is available in the waiting room. Card payments, including contactless, are taken at the desk.

Booking at Bath

You can book online or by phoning the clinic on 01632 960 245. Existing patients in pain should call from 8am for an emergency appointment the same day. New patients are welcome, and the first appointment lasts forty minutes so there is time to talk through your history and any concerns.
