Clear aligners in the UK for American patients

If you started aligner treatment with an orthodontist in the US and are spending a semester or a work posting in England, we can help you keep it on track.

Transferring an existing case

Ask your US provider to transfer your case to our Bath office through the aligner manufacturer's portal. We will review your progress, check how your trays fit, and order any additional aligners you need.

What it costs

A transfer review costs about $120 at current exchange rates. Comprehensive treatment started here costs roughly $3,700 to $5,000, billed in pounds. US orthodontic insurance rarely covers care abroad, so check your plan before you start.

Checkups and emergencies

We see aligner patients every six to eight weeks. If an attachment comes off or a tray cracks, call the office and we will usually see you within a few days.

Going home

When you head back to the States, we send your scans and records to your orthodontist so treatment continues without interruption.

Wearing time abroad

Travel, new food and long workdays make it easy to forget your trays. Set a reminder on your phone for after each meal, carry a spare case, and keep your previous set in case one is lost. Wear time matters more than anything else in keeping treatment on schedule.
