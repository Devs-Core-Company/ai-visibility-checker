Dental care for visitors from the United States

Many of our Bath patients are visiting from the US for work or study. You do not need to be a UK resident to be seen.

Booking as a visitor

Call the clinic or book online and mention that you are visiting. Bring your passport or another photo ID and a list of your medications, including their US brand names, which can differ from UK names.

Paying and insurance

We charge in pounds sterling and take US credit cards. We do not bill American insurers directly. We will give you an itemized receipt with treatment codes your insurer can use, and many US plans reimburse emergency care abroad.

Emergencies

If you have a dental emergency while traveling, call the nearest clinic from 8am. Outside our hours, dial 111 for urgent advice; 911 does not work in the UK, so use 999 for life-threatening emergencies.

Continuing care at home

We can email your X-rays and a treatment summary to your dentist in the US so they can continue your care after you return.

Many American visitors are surprised that a private dental visit in the UK costs less than they expect. Fees are listed on our prices page in pounds, and we are happy to give an estimate in dollars at the current exchange rate before you commit.
