Dr Amelia Hart

Principal dentist

Amelia qualified from the University of Bristol in 2007 and worked in NHS practice in Weston-super-Mare before opening Harbourline on Quay Street in 2012. She is registered with the General Dental Council and holds a postgraduate certificate in restorative dentistry.

Her day-to-day work is general and restorative care: fillings, crowns, bridges and the planning of larger treatments that involve several of our dentists. Patients often mention that she explains options clearly and never rushes a decision.

Outside the practice Amelia teaches part-time on the dental hygiene and therapy course at a local college and volunteers with an oral health programme for care homes in south Bristol.

Amelia sees patients at the Bristol clinic on Mondays, Tuesdays and Thursdays. New patients can request her when booking online or by phone.

Booking an appointment

You can request Dr Amelia Hart when you book online. If you are a new patient, read our guide for new patients first.

Clinic days and how to book

Amelia Hart keeps a number of appointments free each week for new patients. Our receptionists can tell you the next available slot and whether an early or late appointment is possible.

What patients can expect

Every appointment begins with a check on your medical history and a chance to raise anything that is worrying you. Treatment options are explained with photographs or X-rays on the screen, and costs are confirmed in writing before any treatment starts. If you are anxious, say so when you book and extra time will be allowed.

Professional registration

All Harbourline clinicians are registered with the General Dental Council and complete continuing professional development every year. You can check any dental professional on the public GDC register.
