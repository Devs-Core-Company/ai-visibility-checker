Priya Shah

Dental hygienist

Priya trained at the University of Leeds and qualified as a dental hygienist and therapist in 2015. She joined Harbourline when our Leeds clinic opened in 2021.

She treats gum disease, provides scale and polish appointments and places fissure sealants and fluoride varnish for children. Many of her patients are people who have avoided the dentist for years.

Priya believes small, steady changes at home work better than lectures, and she will always show you rather than tell you.

She works at Leeds on Tuesdays, Wednesdays, Fridays and alternate Saturdays.

Booking an appointment

You can request Priya Shah when you book online. If you are a new patient, read our guide for new patients first.

Clinic days and how to book

Priya Shah keeps a number of appointments free each week for new patients. Our receptionists can tell you the next available slot and whether an early or late appointment is possible.

What patients can expect

Every appointment begins with a check on your medical history and a chance to raise anything that is worrying you. Treatment options are explained with photographs or X-rays on the screen, and costs are confirmed in writing before any treatment starts. If you are anxious, say so when you book and extra time will be allowed.

Professional registration

All Harbourline clinicians are registered with the General Dental Council and complete continuing professional development every year. You can check any dental professional on the public GDC register.
