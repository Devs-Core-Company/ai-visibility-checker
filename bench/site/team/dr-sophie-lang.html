Dr Sophie Lang

Orthodontic and cosmetic dentist

Sophie qualified from Cardiff University in 2013 and has focused on aligner orthodontics and cosmetic dentistry since 2017. She runs the Bath clinic and supervises all Invisalign treatment across the practice.

Her approach is conservative: she prefers to straighten and whiten teeth before suggesting veneers, because it keeps more natural tooth. Each cosmetic plan starts with photographs and a digital simulation.

Sophie is a member of the British Society of Restorative Dentistry and attends two clear aligner study days each year.

She works at Bath on Mondays to Thursdays and offers early appointments from 7.30am on Tuesdays.

Booking an appointment

You can request Dr Sophie Lang when you book online. If you are a new patient, read our guide for new patients first.

Clinic days and how to book

Sophie Lang keeps a number of appointments free each week for new patients. Our receptionists can tell you the next available slot and whether an early or late appointment is possible.

What patients can expect

Every appointment begins with a check on your medical history and a chance to raise anything that is worrying you. Treatment options are explained with photographs or X-rays on the screen, and costs are confirmed in writing before any treatment starts. If you are anxious, say so when you book and extra time will be allowed.

Professional registration

All Harbourline clinicians are registered with the General Dental Council and complete continuing professional development every year. You can check any dental professional on the public GDC register.
