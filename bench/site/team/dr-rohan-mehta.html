Dr Rohan Mehta

Implant dentist

Rohan qualified from King’s College London in 2010 and completed a master’s degree in implant dentistry in 2016. He joined Harbourline in 2018 and leads our implant service in Bristol.

He plans every implant case with a 3D scan and a digital guide, which keeps surgery short and accurate. He also takes referrals from other practices for single implants and implant-retained dentures.

Rohan is a keen sailor and spends most summer weekends on the water at Portishead.

He sees implant consultations on Wednesdays and Fridays. A consultation includes the scan and a written plan with fees.

Booking an appointment

You can request Dr Rohan Mehta when you book online. If you are a new patient, read our guide for new patients first.

Clinic days and how to book

Rohan Mehta keeps a number of appointments free each week for new patients. Our receptionists can tell you the next available slot and whether an early or late appointment is possible.

What patients can expect

Every appointment begins with a check on your medical history and a chance to raise anything that is worrying you. Treatment options are explained with photographs or X-rays on the screen, and costs are confirmed in writing before any treatment starts. If you are anxious, say so when you book and extra time will be allowed.

Professional registration

All Harbourline clinicians are registered with the General Dental Council and complete continuing professional development every year. You can check any dental professional on the public GDC register.
