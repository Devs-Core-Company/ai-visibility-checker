Our dental treatments

We offer routine and specialist dental care at all three clinics. Some treatments, such as implants and clear aligners, are based at one clinic, and we will book you there.

    Routine care
  • General dentistry: Check-ups, fillings, root canal treatment and crowns from Harbourline Dental Studio.
  • Dental implants: Single implants, implant bridges and implant-retained dentures placed by Dr Rohan Mehta.
  • Teeth whitening: Dentist-supervised home whitening with custom trays.
  • Invisalign clear aligners: Straighten teeth with near-invisible aligners.
  • Emergency dentist: Same-day emergency dental appointments for toothache, swelling, broken teeth and knocked-out teeth at our Bristol, Bath and Leeds clinics.
  • Hygienist appointments: Scale and polish, gum disease treatment and stain removal with our dental hygienists.
  • Children's dentistry: Gentle dental care for children from their first tooth.
  • Porcelain and composite veneers: Change the shape, colour and length of front teeth with porcelain or composite veneers.

How treatment is planned

Every course of treatment starts with an examination. Your dentist explains the options, including doing nothing, and gives you a written plan with fees. Larger plans are discussed at a separate consultation so there is time for questions.

Where treatment needs skills we do not offer in-house, such as complex oral surgery, we refer you to trusted specialists in Bristol, Bath or Leeds and explain the likely costs before referral.

Children and young people

Children are welcome at every clinic. Our dental therapists provide much of their preventive care, including fluoride varnish and sealants, and children’s check-ups are free when a parent is a registered patient.

Emergency appointments

Every clinic keeps emergency appointments free each weekday morning for patients in pain, with swelling or with a broken tooth. Call from 8am to be seen the same day.
