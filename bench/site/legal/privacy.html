Privacy notice

This notice explains how we use your personal information when you are a patient, when you contact us, and when you visit this website.

Who we are

Harbourline Dental Studio Ltd is the data controller for patient information held at our Bristol, Bath and Leeds clinics. Our data protection lead is the practice manager, who can be contacted at privacy@harbourline.example.

What we collect and why

We hold your contact details, medical history, dental records, X-rays, photographs and payment records so that we can provide safe dental care, keep accurate records as the law requires, and take payment. Health information is special category data, which we process for the purposes of providing health care.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your records, ask us to correct them, and in some cases object to processing or ask for deletion. Clinical records must be kept for set periods and cannot normally be deleted early. You can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office if you are unhappy with how we handle your data.
