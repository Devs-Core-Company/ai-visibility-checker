Terms of treatment

These terms apply to all private treatment at Harbourline clinics. Please read them before your first appointment.

Fees and estimates

We give you a written treatment plan with fees before any course of treatment. If the plan needs to change during treatment, we explain why and confirm the new fee before continuing.

Deposits and payment

Laboratory work such as crowns and veneers requires a deposit of fifty per cent when impressions or scans are taken. The balance is due when the work is fitted. Other treatment is paid for at each appointment.

Cancellations

Please give at least two working days’ notice. Appointments cancelled with less notice, or missed, may be charged at half the fee. We waive this charge for genuine emergencies.

Guarantees

Crowns, bridges, veneers and implant restorations are guaranteed for two years against failure in normal use, provided you attend check-ups and hygiene visits as recommended. The guarantee does not cover damage from accidents or sport without a mouthguard.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with any part of your care, please see our complaints procedure.

Changes to these terms

We review these terms each year. If we change them, the new terms apply to treatment plans issued after the change, and we will tell you about any change that affects treatment you have already agreed.
