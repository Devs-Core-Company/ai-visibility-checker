Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to use this website and our clinics. This statement covers the website at this address.

Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines version 2.2 at level AA. Pages use proper headings, every image has a text alternative, and the site can be used with a keyboard alone and with screen readers such as NVDA and VoiceOver.

Requesting another format

If you need information from this site in large print, easy read or audio, email hello@harbourline.example or phone your clinic. We aim to respond within five working days.

Accessibility in our clinics

Our Bristol and Leeds clinics have step-free access throughout. In Bath, treatment rooms are on the first floor and served by a lift. Hearing loops are fitted at every reception desk, and assistance dogs are welcome.

Reporting a problem

If you find a part of the website that is difficult to use, please tell us which page and what went wrong. We record every report and aim to fix problems within one month, and we review the whole site twice a year.

How we test

We check new pages with automated tools and by hand, using a keyboard only, with screen magnification at 400 per cent and with a screen reader on desktop and mobile. Patients who use assistive technology have also reviewed the booking form and the clinic pages for us.

Known limitations

Some older PDF leaflets linked from treatment pages are not fully accessible to screen readers. We are replacing them with web pages during 2026, and in the meantime we can send any leaflet in an accessible format on request.

Videos on this site have captions. A small number of photographs in older articles have short descriptions only; we are reviewing these as we update each article.
