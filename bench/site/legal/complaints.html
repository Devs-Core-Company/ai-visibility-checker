Complaints procedure

We want to hear when something has gone wrong so that we can put it right and learn from it.

How to complain

Speak to any member of the team, phone the practice manager, or write to complaints@harbourline.example. You can complain on behalf of someone else with their permission.

What happens next

We acknowledge every complaint within two working days and aim to give a full response within twenty working days. If the investigation takes longer, we will tell you why and keep you updated.

If you are not satisfied

Private patients can contact the Dental Complaints Service, an independent service funded by the General Dental Council. Concerns about a dental professional’s fitness to practise can be raised directly with the General Dental Council.

Learning from complaints

We review every complaint at our monthly clinical governance meeting and share anonymised lessons with the whole team. A summary of the changes we have made is included in our annual practice report.

Making a complaint will never affect the care you receive from us. If you would prefer to be seen by a different clinician while a complaint is investigated, we will arrange it.
