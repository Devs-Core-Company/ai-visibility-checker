Cookie policy

This website uses a small number of cookies. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Cookies we use

NamePurposeDuration
hl_sessionKeeps the booking form working as you move between stepsSession
hl_consentRemembers your cookie choices12 months
hl_statsCounts visits to each page without identifying you30 days

Controlling cookies

You can change your cookie choices at any time from the link in the footer, or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking the session cookie stops the online booking form from working, but you can still book by phone.

Changes to this policy

We review this policy whenever we add or remove a cookie, and at least once a year. The date of the last review is shown at the bottom of this page.

Analytics without tracking

Our visit counter is hosted by us, stores no personal information and does not share data with third parties. It tells us which advice articles are read most so we can keep them up to date.

Last reviewed: 1 September 2026.

Third parties

This website does not load advertising, social media or analytics scripts from other companies. Embedded maps on clinic pages are served from our own server rather than from a mapping provider.
