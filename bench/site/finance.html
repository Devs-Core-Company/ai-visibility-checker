Paying for your treatment

You pay for treatment as it happens, and larger courses of treatment can be spread over twelve months without interest.

Paying at each visit

We take debit cards, credit cards and Apple Pay at reception. We do not accept cheques. You receive an itemised receipt by email after every payment.

Interest-free finance

For treatment plans over £1,000 you can pay a ten per cent deposit and spread the remaining cost over up to twelve monthly payments with no interest. Finance is provided by a regulated lender and is subject to a credit check. Your treatment coordinator will help you apply online in about ten minutes.

Example

For a single implant costing £2,650, you would pay a deposit of £265 and then twelve monthly payments of about £198.75. The total amount repayable is £2,650.

Dental insurance and cash plans

Many insurance and cash plans contribute towards check-ups and treatment. We provide the itemised receipts and treatment codes your provider needs, and you claim directly from them.

Refunds

If you pay in advance for a course of treatment and then decide not to continue, we refund any amount paid for treatment not yet carried out, less laboratory costs already incurred. Refunds are made to the card you paid with, normally within ten working days.

Deposits for laboratory work

Crowns, bridges, veneers and dentures are made by a dental laboratory. We ask for a deposit of half the fee when the scan or impression is taken, with the balance due when the work is fitted. If the work cannot be fitted for clinical reasons, the deposit is refunded less any laboratory costs already incurred.
