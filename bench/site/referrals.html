Referring a patient to Harbourline

We accept referrals from other dental practices for implants, clear aligners and gum treatment. Patients return to you for routine care when treatment is finished.

What we accept

Implant referrals go to Dr Rohan Mehta in Bristol. Aligner referrals go to Dr Sophie Lang in Bath. Gum disease referrals for patients with pockets of six millimetres or more go to our Leeds hygiene team, with dentist oversight.

How to refer

Email a referral letter with the patient’s contact details, medical history, the reason for referral and recent radiographs to referrals@harbourline.example. We acknowledge every referral within two working days.

What you receive

You receive a copy of our assessment and treatment plan, a letter when treatment starts and a full report at the end, including maintenance advice and any review we recommend.

Waiting times

Implant consultations are usually available within three weeks. Aligner consultations in Bath are typically available within two weeks. Urgent gum referrals are seen within ten working days.

Communication

Each referring practice has a named contact at Harbourline. You can phone or email them to discuss a case before referring, to check progress, or to ask for copies of radiographs and photographs. We also run two evening study sessions a year for local dentists on implant and aligner case selection, which count towards verifiable CPD.

Sharing care

Referred patients remain your patients. We only carry out the treatment requested in the referral unless we find something urgent, in which case we contact you before acting. When treatment is complete, we send the patient back with clear maintenance instructions and any recommended review intervals.
