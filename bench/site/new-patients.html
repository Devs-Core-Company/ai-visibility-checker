Joining Harbourline as a new patient

We are accepting new patients at all three clinics. Your first appointment is with a dentist and lasts forty minutes.

Your first appointment

We start with your medical history and any concerns, then examine your teeth, gums and mouth and take X-rays if needed. Your dentist explains what they find and gives you a written plan with any treatment and fees. The appointment costs £75.

What to bring

Bring a list of any medicines you take and details of any recent hospital treatment. If you have had treatment elsewhere recently, the name of your previous practice helps us request records.

Medical history

Before your first visit we email a short medical history form. It asks about medicines, allergies, heart conditions, diabetes, pregnancy and anything else that could affect dental treatment. Completing it at home saves about ten minutes at the appointment and lets the dentist read it beforehand.

Fees for new patients

The new patient appointment costs £75 and includes any X-rays that are needed. Children of registered patients are seen free. Any treatment you choose to go ahead with is priced in your written plan before it begins.
