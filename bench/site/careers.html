Working at Harbourline

We employ twenty-two people across three clinics. Most of our team have been with us for more than five years.

Current vacancies

We are recruiting a qualified dental nurse for our Leeds clinic, four days a week, and a part-time receptionist for Bath covering Tuesday and Thursday mornings. Both roles are permanent.

Pay and benefits

We pay above the local average for each role and review pay every April. Benefits include free dental treatment for staff and their children, a paid birthday day off, thirty-three days of annual leave including bank holidays, and a contribution to professional registration fees.

Training

Every team member has a personal training budget each year and protected time to use it. Trainee dental nurses are supported through their qualification, and several of our nurses have gone on to train as hygienists and therapists with our help.

How to apply

Send a short covering letter and your CV to careers@harbourline.example, saying which role and clinic you are interested in. We reply to every application within ten working days and interview at the clinic where the role is based.

Our recruitment process

Shortlisted candidates are invited to an informal visit before the interview, so you can meet the team and see the clinic working. Interviews last about forty-five minutes and are held with the clinic lead and the practice manager. For clinical roles there is a short practical discussion rather than a test. We confirm decisions within a week of the interview.

What it is like to work here

Appointments are booked with realistic time so that nobody has to rush, lunch breaks are protected and the clinics close on time. We hold a team meeting each month where anyone can raise ideas, and many of the changes made at the practice in recent years started as suggestions from nurses and receptionists.
