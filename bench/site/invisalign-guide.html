Your guide to clear aligner treatment

This guide answers the questions we hear at aligner consultations in our Bath clinic, from how many hours a day you wear the trays to what happens after treatment ends.

Wearing your aligners

Aligners only move teeth while they are in. Wear them for twenty to twenty-two hours a day and take them out only to eat, drink anything other than cold water, and clean your teeth. Most people find the first two days of each new set feel tight.

Attachments and refinements

Small tooth-coloured bumps called attachments are bonded to some teeth so the aligners can grip them. Towards the end, a fresh scan often leads to a short series of refinement aligners to perfect the result. These are included in our fee.

Cost in the UK

Treatment starts at £2,900 for mild cases and £3,900 for comprehensive treatment, including retainers and all reviews. Interest-free finance over twelve months is available, subject to status.

After treatment

Teeth tend to drift back. We fit a bonded wire retainer behind the front teeth and a clear removable retainer for night-time wear, which you should keep using indefinitely.

Reading from the US? See the US edition of this guide.

Is it painful?

Most people describe pressure rather than pain for a day or two after each new set. Over-the-counter painkillers are rarely needed after the first week, and the aligners themselves are smooth and do not rub like brackets.
