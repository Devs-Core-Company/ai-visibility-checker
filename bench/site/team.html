Registration and training

All our dentists, hygienists and therapists are registered with the General Dental Council. Each completes at least fifty hours of verifiable professional development every five years for dentists and seventy-five for hygienists and therapists, as the GDC requires, and many do considerably more.

We hold team training days twice a year on medical emergencies, safeguarding and infection control, and all clinical staff renew their life support training annually.

Joining the team

We are often looking for experienced dental nurses and receptionists. See our careers page for current roles.

Dental nurses and reception

Behind every appointment is a dental nurse who prepares the surgery, sterilises instruments and helps during treatment, and a reception team who manage bookings and payments. Many of our nurses hold extra qualifications in radiography, oral health education or sedation nursing, and several have worked with us for more than ten years. You will meet them at every visit, and they are always happy to answer questions about appointments and aftercare.
