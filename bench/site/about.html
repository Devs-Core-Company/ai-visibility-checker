About Harbourline

Dr Amelia Hart opened Harbourline on Quay Street in Bristol in 2012 with two surgeries, one hygienist and a receptionist who still works with us today.

How we work

We are an independent, privately owned practice. That means decisions about appointment lengths, equipment and training are made by the clinicians who see patients, not by a corporate group. We keep appointments long enough to do the job properly and to answer questions.

We added the Bath clinic in 2016 and Leeds in 2021. Each clinic has its own lead dentist, and patients can move between sites without repeating tests because records are shared securely.

Our principal dentist

Meet Dr Amelia Hart, who founded the practice and still sees patients three days a week. You can also meet the rest of our team.

Our commitments

Every treatment plan is written down with fees before treatment starts. We publish our prices online, we answer complaints within two working days, and we take part in the Dental Complaints Service scheme for private patients.

We are inspected by the Care Quality Commission like every dental practice in England, and the inspection reports for each clinic are available from the CQC website.

Our team today

Twenty-two people now work across the three clinics: five dentists, four hygienists and therapists, eight dental nurses and a reception and management team. Most have been with us for more than five years, which is one reason patients tell us they see familiar faces at each visit.

We train dental nurses from scratch, support nurses who want to become hygienists, and host dental students from Bristol for observation placements twice a year.
