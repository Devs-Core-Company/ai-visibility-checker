Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions our receptionists hear most often. If your question is not here, please contact us.

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. All three clinics are accepting new private patients. New patient appointments last forty minutes and cost £75, including any X-rays needed.

Do you offer NHS treatment?

No. Harbourline is a private practice. We do not hold an NHS contract, but we keep our fees published and offer interest-free finance on larger treatments.

How much is a check-up?

A routine check-up with a dentist costs £55. A hygiene appointment costs £70 for thirty minutes or £95 for forty-five minutes.

Can I be seen today if I am in pain?

Usually, yes. We hold emergency appointments every weekday morning. Call the clinic from 8am. An emergency appointment costs £85 plus any treatment.

Do you see children?

Yes, from their first tooth. Check-ups for children under eighteen are free when a parent or guardian is a registered patient.

Is there parking?

Bristol patients can park free for two hours at Trenchard Street car park. Bath has no parking; Southgate car park is closest. Leeds is a short walk from the railway station.

How do I pay?

We accept debit and credit cards and Apple Pay. Payment is due at each appointment. Treatment over £1,000 can be spread over twelve months interest-free, subject to status.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give us at least two working days’ notice. Appointments missed or cancelled with less notice may be charged at half the appointment fee.
