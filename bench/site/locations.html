Find a Harbourline clinic

We have three clinics. You can be seen at any of them, and your records are shared securely between sites.

  • Bristol, Quay Street
  • Bath, Abbey Green
  • Leeds, Granary Wharf

Which clinic should I choose?

Most people choose the clinic nearest home or work. Implant surgery takes place in Bristol, and clear aligner treatment is based in Bath, but check-ups and hygiene visits for those patients can happen at any site.

Accessibility

Bristol and Leeds have step-free access to every room. Bath is in a listed building; treatment rooms are on the first floor and reached by a lift. Please tell us when you book if you need extra time or help.

Moving between clinics

Your dental records, X-rays and treatment plans are held on one secure system, so you can switch clinic at any time without repeating tests. Just tell reception which site you would prefer for future visits.

Opening hours

Bristol is open Monday to Friday from 8am to 7pm and on Saturday mornings. Bath opens from 7.30am Monday to Thursday and closes at 4pm on Fridays. Leeds is open Tuesday to Friday and on alternate Saturday mornings. Each clinic page lists exact hours, travel directions and parking.
