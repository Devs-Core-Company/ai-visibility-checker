Visiting the dental hygienist

A hygiene appointment removes the hard tartar that brushing cannot shift and gives you practical advice for keeping gums healthy between visits.

What happens at the appointment

The hygienist measures the gum pockets, removes tartar above and below the gum line with an ultrasonic scaler and fine hand instruments, then polishes away surface stain. Most visits take thirty to forty-five minutes.

If the gums bleed easily or the pockets are deeper than four millimetres, the hygienist will suggest a course of deeper cleaning over two or three visits.

How often to go

People with healthy gums usually need a visit every six to twelve months. Smokers, people with diabetes and anyone treated for gum disease benefit from visits every three to four months.

Advice you can use at home

Your hygienist will show you the right size of interdental brush for each gap and check your brushing technique. Small changes in technique make a larger difference than any particular brand of toothpaste.

Who provides hygienist appointments

Priya Shah and our hygiene team leads this service, which is available at all three clinics. Every clinician who treats you is registered with the General Dental Council, and you can ask to see the same person at each visit.

Your first appointment

We start with a conversation about what you would like to change and any worries you have, followed by an examination. You will leave with a written plan that sets out the options, how long each takes and the fee for each stage. There is no obligation to go ahead, and you can take the plan away to think about it.

Questions worth asking

Ask what the alternatives are, including doing nothing, how long the result is expected to last, and what the follow-up visits involve. Our team would much rather answer questions before treatment than after it.

Fees and booking

The current fee for hygienist appointments is listed on our prices page, and your dentist will confirm it in writing before treatment. You can book online or call any of our clinics.
