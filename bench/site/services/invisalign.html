Invisalign clear aligners

Clear aligners straighten crowded or spaced teeth with a series of thin plastic trays changed every one to two weeks. Dr Sophie Lang plans and supervises aligner treatment at our Bath clinic.

Is it right for you

Aligners work well for mild to moderate crowding, gaps and some bite problems. Severe rotations or large bite corrections may need fixed braces, and we will tell you plainly if that is the better option.

What treatment involves

After a digital scan you see a simulation of how your teeth will move. Aligners must be worn for twenty to twenty-two hours a day. Most adult cases take six to eighteen months, followed by retainers to hold the result.

We review progress every six to eight weeks and add small tooth-coloured attachments where teeth need extra grip.

Who provides invisalign clear aligners

Dr Sophie Lang leads this service, which is available at Bath. Every clinician who treats you is registered with the General Dental Council, and you can ask to see the same person at each visit.

Your first appointment

We start with a conversation about what you would like to change and any worries you have, followed by an examination. You will leave with a written plan that sets out the options, how long each takes and the fee for each stage. There is no obligation to go ahead, and you can take the plan away to think about it.

Questions worth asking

Ask what the alternatives are, including doing nothing, how long the result is expected to last, and what the follow-up visits involve. Our team would much rather answer questions before treatment than after it.

Fees and booking

The current fee for invisalign clear aligners is listed on our prices page, and your dentist will confirm it in writing before treatment. You can book online or call any of our clinics.
