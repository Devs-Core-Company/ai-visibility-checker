General dentistry for the whole family

Most visits to Harbourline are for everyday care: a check-up, a filling, a crown or advice about a tooth that has started to ache. Our general dentists see adults and children at all three clinics and keep appointments to time.

Check-ups are available at all three clinics during normal opening hours. Early and late slots are popular, so book a few weeks ahead if you need one.

What a check-up includes

A routine examination takes about twenty minutes. Your dentist checks each tooth for decay, measures the gum pockets around them, looks at the soft tissues of the mouth for early signs of disease and asks about any changes since your last visit.

We take bitewing X-rays every one to two years depending on your risk of decay. You see the images on the screen in the surgery and your dentist explains anything that needs attention before any treatment is booked.

Fillings and crowns

We use tooth-coloured composite for most fillings. Large cavities in back teeth may need an onlay or a crown, which we make with a local laboratory in Bristol and fit at a second appointment two weeks later.

Each treatment plan lists the fee for every item before you agree to it, so there are no surprises at the reception desk.

Root canal treatment

When the nerve of a tooth becomes infected, root canal treatment can save the tooth. Single-rooted teeth usually take one visit of about an hour. Molars can take two visits, and complex cases are referred to our endodontist in Bath.

Who provides general dentistry

Dr Amelia Hart leads this service, which is available at all three clinics. Every clinician who treats you is registered with the General Dental Council, and you can ask to see the same person at each visit.

Your first appointment

We start with a conversation about what you would like to change and any worries you have, followed by an examination. You will leave with a written plan that sets out the options, how long each takes and the fee for each stage. There is no obligation to go ahead, and you can take the plan away to think about it.

Questions worth asking

Ask what the alternatives are, including doing nothing, how long the result is expected to last, and what the follow-up visits involve. Our team would much rather answer questions before treatment than after it.

Fees and booking

The current fee for general dentistry is listed on our prices page, and your dentist will confirm it in writing before treatment. You can book online or call any of our clinics.
