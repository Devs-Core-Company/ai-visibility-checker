Dental implants that look and work like natural teeth

An implant is a small titanium post placed in the jawbone that supports a crown, a bridge or a denture. Dr Rohan Mehta places implants at our Bristol clinic and restores them with crowns made by our partner laboratory.

Who is suitable

Most healthy adults with enough bone in the jaw can have implants. Heavy smoking, uncontrolled diabetes and active gum disease all reduce the chance of success, so we treat or manage those first.

A 3D scan at your consultation shows the height and width of bone available. If there is not enough, a bone graft can be placed at the same time or a few months before the implant.

The treatment timeline

Placing the implant takes about an hour under local anaesthetic. The bone then bonds to the implant over eight to twelve weeks, during which you wear a temporary tooth. The final crown is fitted once the implant is stable.

Looking after implants

Implants cannot decay but the gum around them can become inflamed. Clean them as carefully as natural teeth, use interdental brushes daily and keep your hygiene appointments every six months.

Who provides dental implants

Dr Rohan Mehta leads this service, which is available at Bristol. Every clinician who treats you is registered with the General Dental Council, and you can ask to see the same person at each visit.

Your first appointment

We start with a conversation about what you would like to change and any worries you have, followed by an examination. You will leave with a written plan that sets out the options, how long each takes and the fee for each stage. There is no obligation to go ahead, and you can take the plan away to think about it.

Questions worth asking

Ask what the alternatives are, including doing nothing, how long the result is expected to last, and what the follow-up visits involve. Our team would much rather answer questions before treatment than after it.

Fees and booking

The current fee for dental implants is listed on our prices page, and your dentist will confirm it in writing before treatment. You can book online or call any of our clinics.
