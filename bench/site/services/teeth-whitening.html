Professional teeth whitening

Whitening lightens the natural colour of teeth that have darkened with age, coffee, tea or red wine. In the UK only dentists may provide whitening products above 0.1% hydrogen peroxide, and we supervise every course from start to finish.

How home whitening works

We take a scan of your teeth and make thin, clear trays that fit closely. You wear the trays with a carbamide peroxide gel for an hour a day or overnight for two to three weeks.

Most people see a change of several shades. We record your starting shade so you can compare at the review appointment.

Sensitivity and safety

Some people notice short-lived sensitivity during whitening. Using a fluoride toothpaste for sensitive teeth and skipping a night or two usually settles it. Whitening does not change the colour of crowns, veneers or fillings.

Who provides teeth whitening

Dr Sophie Lang leads this service, which is available at Bath and Bristol. Every clinician who treats you is registered with the General Dental Council, and you can ask to see the same person at each visit.

Your first appointment

We start with a conversation about what you would like to change and any worries you have, followed by an examination. You will leave with a written plan that sets out the options, how long each takes and the fee for each stage. There is no obligation to go ahead, and you can take the plan away to think about it.

Questions worth asking

Ask what the alternatives are, including doing nothing, how long the result is expected to last, and what the follow-up visits involve. Our team would much rather answer questions before treatment than after it.

Fees and booking

The current fee for teeth whitening is listed on our prices page, and your dentist will confirm it in writing before treatment. You can book online or call any of our clinics.
