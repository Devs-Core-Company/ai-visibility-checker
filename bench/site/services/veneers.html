Veneers for chipped, worn or discoloured teeth

A veneer is a thin shell bonded to the front of a tooth. Veneers can close small gaps, lengthen worn teeth and cover stains that whitening cannot shift.

Composite or porcelain

Composite veneers are built up directly on the tooth in one visit and can be repaired easily. Porcelain veneers are made in a laboratory, resist staining better and usually last longer, but need two visits and some reshaping of the tooth.

Trial smile

Before any tooth is prepared we place a temporary mock-up so you can see the proposed shape and length in your own mouth and ask for changes.

Caring for veneers

Avoid biting hard objects such as pens or ice. If you grind your teeth at night we will make a protective night guard to wear over the veneers.

Who provides porcelain and composite veneers

Dr Sophie Lang leads this service, which is available at Bath. Every clinician who treats you is registered with the General Dental Council, and you can ask to see the same person at each visit.

Your first appointment

We start with a conversation about what you would like to change and any worries you have, followed by an examination. You will leave with a written plan that sets out the options, how long each takes and the fee for each stage. There is no obligation to go ahead, and you can take the plan away to think about it.

Questions worth asking

Ask what the alternatives are, including doing nothing, how long the result is expected to last, and what the follow-up visits involve. Our team would much rather answer questions before treatment than after it.

Fees and booking

The current fee for porcelain and composite veneers is listed on our prices page, and your dentist will confirm it in writing before treatment. You can book online or call any of our clinics.
